The problem

The average person juggles 5–7 accounts with no unified view and no one acting on their behalf. Tools either inform you (Mint, Copilot) or execute in one silo — nobody acts across your whole financial life.

  • Idle cash left in low-yield checking, forgotten subscriptions, missed tax windows
  • Quality advice often requires six-figure minimums with an RIA
  • Banks are conflicted — their AI won’t tell you to move money to a competitor

One agent, full picture

An AI agent that monitors your complete financial picture and executes within guardrails you set.

Unified view + execution

Sweeps idle cash, cancels unused subs, optimizes bill pay, flags tax-loss windows — coordinated across accounts, not one app at a time.

Guardrails you control

Set limits once. Low-stakes, reversible actions run automatically; bigger moves wait for your clear approval flow.

Tiered autonomy

Automatic moves, one-tap approvals for medium stakes, and full analysis when you — or a human advisor — need to make the call.

How it works

Three levels of agency. One product.

Tier 1 — Automatic

Low-stakes, reversible actions the agent runs on its own: cash sweeps, subscription cleanup, bill pay — within the guardrails you set once.

Tier 2 — One-tap approve

Medium-stakes moves prepared end-to-end. You review a concise summary and approve with a single tap.

Tier 3 — You decide

High-stakes decisions — RSUs, refinancing, tax strategy. The agent builds the full analysis; you or a human advisor makes the call.

Why now

Open banking APIs (e.g. Plaid) with read + write across thousands of institutions, LLMs that reason over multi-variable money decisions, and real-time rails (FedNow, RTP) for movement when it matters.
